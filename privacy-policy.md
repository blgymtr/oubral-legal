# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 9 November 2026 (adds Instagram and TikTok)*

This policy covers **Oubral's Lantern Uploader** ("the App"), a private desktop tool used only by the developer of the game Oubral's Lantern to upload and schedule the game's own videos to the game's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, through each platform's official API, and to see how those videos are doing.

## 1. Who uses the App
The App is used only by the channel owner, on the owner's own computer, with the owner's own Google, Instagram and TikTok accounts. It is not distributed to or used by anyone else.

## 2. What Google data the App accesses
With the owner's consent, the App requests these Google OAuth scopes:

- `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload` — to upload videos and their thumbnails to the owner's channel and set their scheduled publication time.
- `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly` — to confirm the correct channel was authorised and to read the status and statistics of the owner's own videos.

The App reads only the minimum it needs: the channel's name, the status of videos it uploaded, and the statistics of the channel and its recent videos, such as subscriber and view counts.

## 2a. Instagram (Meta) data the App accesses
With the owner's consent, the App uses the Instagram API to publish Reels to the owner's own Instagram professional account (@oubralslantern). Permissions: `instagram_basic` (account name, media list) and `instagram_content_publish` (publish the owner's own videos). It reads only the account name, the status and links of posts it published, and, if granted, their view and engagement counts.

## 2b. TikTok data the App accesses
With the owner's consent, the App uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API to post videos to the owner's own TikTok account (@oubralslantern). Scopes: `user.info.basic` (display name and avatar, to confirm the correct account), `video.upload` and `video.publish` (upload and post the owner's own videos). It reads only the creator information TikTok requires before posting and the status of the posts it made.

## 3. How the data is used
The data is used solely to upload the game's videos, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publication times, confirm that uploads succeeded, and show the owner the channel's statistics on the owner's own computer. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

## 4. Storage and security
Access and refresh tokens are stored only in local files on the owner's computer. They are never sent to any server other than the issuing platform's (Google, Meta or TikTok). Upload records and the statistics history are also kept only on the owner's computer. The App has no server, database or cloud storage of its own.

## 5. Sharing
The App does not sell, share, transfer or disclose any user data from Google, Meta or TikTok to any third party.

## 6. Retention and deletion
Tokens and local records remain on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time at [Google Account permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions), in Instagram under Settings > Apps and websites, and in TikTok under Settings > Security > Manage app permissions; deleting the local files removes all stored data.

## 7. YouTube and Google
By using the App, the owner is bound by the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Google's handling of data is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## 7a. Instagram and TikTok
Use of the App with Instagram is subject to the [Meta Platform Terms](https://developers.facebook.com/terms) and the [Instagram Terms of Use](https://help.instagram.com/581066165581870). Use with TikTok is subject to the [TikTok Terms of Service](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/terms-of-service) and the [TikTok Developer Terms](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/tiktok-developer-terms-of-service).
